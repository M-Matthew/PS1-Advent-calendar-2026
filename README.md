# PS1-Advent-calendar-2026
This app is a PlayStation 1 advent calendar. On December 2026 you can click on the each calendar's door, listen to the music and guess which game is the music from. Will you be able to guess all the 24 musics?

## Description
This Android app is the real reason why I started almost 1 year ago to study C#. I told me, I had to develop this app before the end of the 2026. I can now inform you, I am real happy. I did much more than I expeced, beacause 3 android apps are already published and during this adventure I learned so much about C# and NET MAUI.
This app is made for the PS1 nostalgics and works like an advent calendar. Simply click on the corresponding door, listen to the music and guess which game is the music from. You will also find a small description of the right game.
Will you be able to guess all the 24 musics? Will you be patient till the 1st December 2026? Don't forget: this app includes a surprise: will you be able to find it?

## Properties
- Download and install the .APK file on your device.
- The app runs on Android systems only.
- The app is performed for smartphones, but it also runs well on tablet.
- This app has been tested with VirusTotal. Results are at the page bottom.
